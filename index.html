03 / BODY KITS AND CAR PARTS

Body kits made for your car.

Front lips, rear diffusers, ducktail spoilers and side skirts, designed to fit your exact make and model, from a BMW M4 to a daily driver. We also make shift knobs, vent louvres, trim inserts, clips and mounts. Tell us what you want and we will design it, quote it and print it.

Concept renders of kit pieces on a grey fitting buck. Carbon-look finish shown; parts are printed in PETG or ASA.

How to request a part

  1. Tell us the car. Make, model and year, plus trim if it matters for fit.
  2. Describe the part in detail. Where it goes, what it replaces or adds, rough size, finish and any measurements you have.
  3. Add a photo or file if you have one. A photo of the spot it fits, a sketch, or a CAD file all help.
  4. Get a quote. We reply by email with a design plan and price before anything is printed.

Good to know: parts are printed in heat-resistant PETG or ASA for car use. They are for cosmetic and comfort parts, not for structural, safety or engine-bay parts. Large pieces are printed in sections and joined.

05 / QUESTIONS

Good to know

How does the student discount work?

Enter your school email at checkout. If it is a recognized school address, 10% comes off your items. We confirm eligibility before accepting the order.

What does shipping cost?

Shipping is a flat $12 CAD anywhere in Canada. Pickup in the Greater Toronto Area is free.

How long will my order take?

Most orders are printed within 3 to 7 days of payment. Shipping time is extra.

How are custom prints priced?

It depends on size, print time and material. Upload your file and we email a quote before anything is printed. Student discount applies.

What car parts can you make?

Body kit pieces such as front lips, diffusers, ducktail spoilers and side skirts, plus shift knobs, vent louvres, trim inserts, clips and mounts. We do not make structural, safety or engine-bay parts. Send your make, model, year and a detailed description for a quote.

What are the items made of?

PLA plastic. Display pieces and light-duty tools only, so keep them away from heat and direct sun.
